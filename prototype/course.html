Introduction to the course

In this course Alvaro Castagnet shares his approach to painting interiors and exteriors with expressive watercolor. You will learn how to capture light, atmosphere, and the soul of a place.

What you will need

  • Watercolor paper (300 gsm, cold press)
  • A limited palette: warm and cool primaries
  • Round brushes #8 and #12, flat brush 1 inch
  • Reference photos provided in materials below
Kinescope Main lesson video

Working process

Alvaro recommends starting with loose washes and building structure gradually. Watch the demonstration above, then review the step-by-step notes in the PDF.

Key takeaways

  1. Compose with large shapes first
  2. Reserve whites consciously
  3. Use edges to direct the viewer's eye
