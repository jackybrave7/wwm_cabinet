Materials La Fe uses for the single-colored rose. He paints without a pencil sketch — the form is built in color. Lesson 1 shows his table; lesson 3 shows how he prepares the paper.

  1. Paper. Cotton watercolor paper, taped to a board. La Fe spends a full video on stretching and preparing the sheet — follow that rather than improvising.
  2. Paints. A small set of watercolors. He lets each hue do its own work instead of mixing everything on the palette. The overview video shows the exact colors for this rose.
  3. Brushes. Several rounds in different sizes and a wide flat / hake brush for wetting the sheet.
  4. Palette. A large white mixing tray.
  5. Water. Two jars — one for clean water, one for rinsing — plus a cloth or paper towel.
  6. Tape to hold the paper to the board.
  7. No pencil. La Fe does not draw the rose first. White paper left untouched is part of the painting.
  8. A live rose (or a clear photo) in front of you while you paint.
