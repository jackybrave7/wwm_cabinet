Materials Nono Garcia uses for the ceramic bowl. He paints without a pencil. This lesson adds a short grading exercise before the bowl, then the ornament on the clay.

  1. Paper. Cotton watercolor paper on a board. Have a spare scrap for the grading practice in the second video of this block.
  2. Paints. A very small palette — students often work with only two colors here, a warm earth and a cool grey-blue. Follow the overview video rather than expanding the set.
  3. Brushes. Rounds for the bowl’s volume and a finer point for the ornament. A wider brush for the atmosphere around the bowl.
  4. Palette. A large mixing tray. A water jar and a spray bottle help keep the wash moving.
  5. No pencil. The bowl and the ornament are placed with the brush. Reserved paper is the gloss on the ceramic.
  6. A ceramic bowl (or a clear photo) in front of you — preferably one with a simple ornament you can read in the light.
