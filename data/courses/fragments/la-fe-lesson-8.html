Materials La Fe uses for the two-colored rose. The setup is the same as the first lesson: no pencil, prepared paper, and a live flower in front of him.

  1. Paper. Cotton watercolor paper, taped to a board. Repeat the paper preparation from the first rose if you are starting here.
  2. Paints. A small set of watercolors, with more contrast between the two hues of the petals plus greens for the leaves. The overview video shows the exact colors for this rose.
  3. Brushes. Several rounds in different sizes and a wide flat / hake brush for wetting the sheet.
  4. Palette. A large white mixing tray.
  5. Water. Two jars — one for clean water, one for rinsing — plus a cloth or paper towel.
  6. Tape to hold the paper to the board.
  7. No pencil. La Fe builds the two-colored rose in paint only. Untouched paper is the light.
  8. A live rose (or a clear photo) in front of you while you paint.
