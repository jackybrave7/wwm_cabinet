Materials Elke Memmler uses for the flower painting in this course.

  1. Paper. Elke always uses the big format 56 × 76 Fabriano or HAHNEMUHLE, 600–640 gr. The surface of the paper is heavily glued, smooth and satin, so the paint stays on the surface for a long time and only gradually penetrates into the lower layers, which gives time to react.
  2. Brushes. Elke as a rule uses 5 brushes, both synthetic and natural:
    • The thickest round brush with a thin tip (synthetic) by Elke Memmler — German brand Lineum, released on special order
    • Thick round brush protein (stores a lot of water and paint)
    • Medium round squirrel brush, Kolinsky
    • Thin round school brush (for drawing details)
    • Fleyts (soft)
  3. Paints. Elke uses paints by the South Korean firm MIJELLO. She does not highlight preferred colors and uses the versatility of the full palette.
  4. Special blend. For the landscape, Elke used a mixture she made herself: titanium white and neapolitan yellow, water and some alcohol. It is used to draw trees and create a veil on the picture.
  5. White mascara.
  6. Palette. Elke uses a large plastic palette (sized for thick brushes). It is divided into three parts: yellow and red shades; cold blues and greens; warm browns and greens.
  7. Pencil. Elke usually does not use a simple pencil, as she does not create sketches. If required, she recommends any soft pencil to avoid damaging the paper.
  8. Spray with water.
