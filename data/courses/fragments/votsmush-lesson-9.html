Recommended materials Alexander Votsmush uses in this course. The overview video shows how he lays them out for the cityscape.

  1. Paper. Watercolor paper — recommended brand Arches Satin.
  2. Paints. Watercolor in tubes (recommended brand Schmincke). Mandatory colors: cobalt, ultramarine, umber, sienna, ochre, Naples pink, Naples yellow, cadmium red, black.
  3. White gouache (titanium).
  4. Cuvettes for the colors.
  5. Plastic palette.
  6. Brushes. Large synthetic flutes, large flat kolinsky brushes (Russian and Chinese), and smaller brushes for details (various brands).
  7. Sketchbook or notebook for sketches.
  8. Watercolor pencils: brown, black, red, pink (recommended brand Koh-i-Noor).
  9. Dermatograph pencil (recommended brand Mitsubishi).
  10. Watercolor felt-tip pens (recommended brand Sakura).
  11. Crayons (Koh-i-Noor) and watercolor crayons (Sakura).
  12. Reserve (liquid rubber) based on ammonia.
  13. Board / tablet — plastic is better.
  14. Pencil, eraser (or a kneaded eraser), sharpener, and a water jar.
  15. Paper tape.
