Materials Nono Garcia uses for the metal teapot. He still paints without a pencil — the form is built in tone, and the later parts layer reflections on the silver surface.

  1. Paper. Cotton watercolor paper, taped or clipped to a board, used horizontally.
  2. Paints. A very small set of greys, blues and earths. Nono keeps the palette austere so the metal reads as light, not as decoration. The overview video shows the tubes he opens for this teapot.
  3. Brushes. A few rounds with a fine point for the highlights and ornament on the teapot, plus a wider brush for the first wash and the shadow.
  4. Palette. A simple mixing tray — enough space to keep clean water-greys separate from the darker accents.
  5. Water and a cloth or paper towel. Highlights are the untouched paper, not white paint.
  6. No pencil. Nono draws the teapot with the brush. White paper left untouched is the shine on the metal.
  7. The teapot (or a clear photo) in front of you while you paint — a small silver teapot with a pointed lid.
