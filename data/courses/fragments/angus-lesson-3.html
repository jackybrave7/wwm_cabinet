Materials Angus McEwan uses for the door painting in this course. Lesson 2 shows his working setup in the studio.

  1. Paper. Watercolor paper taped to a board. Angus works on a vertical surface so washes can run; keep paper towels along the bottom edge of the board.
  2. Paints. Angus typically paints with Daniel Smith watercolors. His core palette:
    • Van Dyke Brown
    • Burnt Umber
    • Prussian Blue
    • Cobalt Blue
    • Manganese Blue Hue
    • Lavender
    • Cobalt Teal Blue
    • Indian Yellow
    • Organic Vermillion
    • Quinacridone Burnt Orange
    • Chinese White
  3. Gold acrylic for the bolts.
  4. Watercolor crayons (Caran d’Ache Neocolor II or similar) for texture and light.
  5. Brushes. A range of rounds, including a small pointed brush for the final details.
  6. Sponge to build surface texture.
  7. Cardboard / card to print shapes (the numbers on the door).
  8. Pencil for the preparatory drawing and to restore lines after washes.
  9. Spray bottle with water to smooth and move the texture layers.
  10. Paper tape, paper towels, and a water can.
