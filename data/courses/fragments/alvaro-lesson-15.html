Materials Alvaro Castagnet uses for Red Square on a sunny day. “Every artist should work with the best materials that he can afford.”

  1. Paper. Arches or Saunders Waterford 300 g, rough texture, off-white. According to Alvaro Castagnet, paper of worthy quality matters not only for the result and the pleasure of painting, but also for the long-term preservation of watercolor works. He prefers large sheets; in the video he works in A2.
  2. Paints. Alvaro uses Daniel Smith:
    • Alizarin crimson
    • Hard burn cyan
    • Ultramarine blue
    • Yellow ochre
    • Lavender
    • Cobalt
    • Cadmium red
    • Viridian
    • Cerulean
    • Neutral tint
    • Dark scarlet (sometimes)
    • Cadmium yellow
    Alvaro always keeps one warm and one cold shade of each color — for example warm and cool yellow, red, and blue.
  3. Brushes. “I believe that the main instrument on which the work of the artist depends is the brush. Natural brushes are good for painting, soft and absorbing the moisture well,” — Alvaro Castagnet. He uses Escoda (Spain) brushes he helped design: squirrel hair, a fairly long nap, and a pointed tip that holds water and keeps a sharp point.
    • Alvaro Castagnet brush No. 10 (round)
    • Alvaro Castagnet brush No. 6 (round)
    • Alvaro Castagnet brush No. 4 (round)
    • Alvaro Castagnet brush No. 2 (round)
    • Alvaro Castagnet brush «needle point» No. 8
  4. Pencil. A simple collet pencil, 6B (soft).
  5. Palette. A brass metal palette covered with white enamel.
  6. Water bottle.
  7. Paper tape.
  8. Paper towels.
  9. Water can.
  10. Sponge.
  11. Hair dryer to speed up the work.
