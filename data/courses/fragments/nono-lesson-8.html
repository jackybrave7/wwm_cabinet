Materials Nono Garcia uses for the glass still life. The setup is the same idea as the bowl: no pencil, a small palette, and the white of the paper as light inside the glass.

  1. Paper. Cotton watercolor paper on a board. Keep the sheet clean — the glass is mostly reserved whites and very light washes.
  2. Paints. A short range of cool greys and a touch of warmer earth for the reflections. The overview video shows the exact colors for this glass.
  3. Brushes. A pointed round for the rim and the thin walls, and a wider brush for the first pale wash and the shadows on the table.
  4. Palette and two jars of water — one clean, one for rinsing — plus a cloth or tissue to lift paint.
  5. No pencil. The glass is drawn with the brush. Untouched paper is the highlight and the water.
  6. A clear glass (or a photo) in front of you, with daylight so you can see the shadows through the water.
